Help & sign conventions

Units (SI)

All input in SI base units: length [m], force [N], moment [N·m], distributed load [N/m], modulus E & G [Pa], area I [m⁴], area A [m²], Poisson ratio ν and shear factor κ dimensionless. Results are returned in the same units.

Sign conventions

  • A downward point force or distributed load is positive (up is negative).
  • Deflection w is positive downward; slope dw/dx and rotation φ follow the standard beam sign.
  • Bending moment is reported so that a sagging (tension on the bottom fibre) beam is negative — the classic “smile” is below the axis. Reactions: vertical is + up, moment is + counter-clockwise.
  • Distributed loads taper linearly from Value 1 (at start) to Value 2 (at end).

Theories

  • Euler–Bernoulli: classic bending theory, no shear deformation. Best for slender beams (length ≫ depth).
  • Timoshenko: adds shear deformation via the shear area Aₛ = κ·A and shear modulus G. Use for short / deep beams where shear shortening is significant. Leave G blank to derive it as E / 2(1+ν); leave κ blank for the 5/6 rectangular default.

Boundary conditions

  • Free — no restraint (a cantilever tip).
  • Pinned — hinge / roller: deflection restrained, rotation free.
  • Sliding (guided) — rotation restrained, vertical deflection free.
  • Fixed — clamp: both deflection and rotation restrained.

Tips

  • Increase Elements per break-segment for accuracy on distributed / tapered loads — a single element under a UDL is noticeably soft.
  • A model with no support and no fixed/guided end is a rigid-body mode and will be reported as unstable. Add ≥ one restraint.
